## Description (max 4000)
A quiet place to shop exceptional things.

Yu+Mi is designed for neurodivergent men who feel the world deeply. Every object
here has a second job: to soften something, to steady something, or to give your
hands somewhere calm to be.

THE COLLECTION
Fashion — tactile-considered menswear: cashmere, silk, soft tailoring.
Fashion Accessories — iced moissanite jewellery and designer-grade eyewear.
Personal Electronics — stimulus-shaping audio, wearables and focus devices.
Bedding & Sanctuary — weighted, soft, deeply regulating bedding.
Objects & Play — high-end fidget, puzzle and focus objects, made properly.

QUIET BY DESIGN
No countdown timers. No pop-ups. No noise. A hushed, tactile interface with
material and sensory notes on every product, plain-language sizing, and secure
checkout powered by Shopify.
